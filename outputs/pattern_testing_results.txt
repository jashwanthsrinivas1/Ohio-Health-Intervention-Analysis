--- Key Finding ---
Interventions performed best when the metric was CLOSEST to target.
As distance from target increased, average outcomes declined.

This supports the recommendation to use distance from target as a
primary filter in the control panel decision logic.

--- Recovery Threshold Test ---
When observed recovery EXCEEDED the triggering gap:
  -> Post-intervention outcomes were consistently stronger.

When observed recovery did NOT exceed the triggering gap:
  -> Outcomes were mixed with no clear positive signal.

============================================================================
Conclusion: Intervention value depends on WHEN action is taken,
not just WHETHER action is taken.
